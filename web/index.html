How ASKME Works

Four deterministic steps turning raw citizen evidence into formal statutory petitions.

01. Multimodal Evidence Intake
EXIF GPS extraction, voice acoustic transcription, and photo categorization.
02. GIS Statutory Routing
Locks onto 19 verified authorities (NHAI, GCC, BBMP, Indian Railways RailMadad).
03. Legal Citation Engine
Embeds Indian Penal Code, Municipal Corporation Acts, and National Green Tribunal precedents.
04. Citizen Vault Persistence
On-device encrypted history tracking with formal PDF, Email, and WhatsApp dispatch.
